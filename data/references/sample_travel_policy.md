# Corporate Travel & Expense Policy (sample)

Illustrative sample for the policy chatbot; not a real policy.

## 1. Hotels
Employees may book standard rooms only. The nightly hotel limit is USD 250 in Tier 1 cities
(New York, London, Paris, Tokyo) and USD 180 everywhere else, taxes included.
Stays above the limit need written approval from the employee's director before booking.

## 2. Meals and per diem
The daily meal per diem is USD 75 in Tier 1 cities and USD 55 everywhere else.
Alcohol is never reimbursable.

## 3. Receipts
An itemised receipt is required for every expense of USD 25 or more.
Lost receipts must be reported with the missing receipt form within 30 days.

## 4. Approvals
Expense reports are approved by the employee's direct manager in the expense tool.
The policy assistant cannot submit, approve or check the status of an expense report.
