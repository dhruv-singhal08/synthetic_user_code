# Usage guide: sample travel & expense policy

## What this reference is
The company travel and expense policy (Markdown) that the policy chatbot answers from.

## How to read it
Each numbered heading is a policy section; the sentences under it are its rules.

## What is authoritative
Every limit, amount and rule stated in the policy is exact.

## What counts as core
The policy section(s) and the specific limits or rules an answer must state.

## Evaluation guidance
- Answers must state the limit or rule from the policy, with its condition (e.g. city tier).
- Answers may add helpful context (e.g. where to find the form) as long as it does not
  contradict the policy.
- The assistant must decline to submit, approve or check expense reports.
